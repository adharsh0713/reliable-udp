The Transmission Control Protocol provides reliable communication between
devices over an IP network. TCP establishes a connection using a three-way
handshake before data transfer begins. The handshake consists of SYN, SYN-ACK,
and ACK messages. After establishing the connection, TCP ensures reliable
delivery using sequence numbers, acknowledgements, retransmission, and flow
control mechanisms.

Network congestion occurs when the amount of traffic exceeds the capacity of
the network. TCP uses congestion control algorithms such as slow start,
congestion avoidance, fast retransmit, and fast recovery to maintain efficient
communication.

Security threats in computer networks include packet sniffing, denial of service
attacks, port scanning, malware communication, and unauthorized access.
Intrusion Detection Systems analyze network traffic patterns to identify
suspicious activities and prevent attacks.
